TRAVEL RESPONSIBLY

🌱 Sustainable Travel in UP

Help preserve Uttar Pradesh’s centuries-old monuments, sacred rivers, and natural habitats for future generations.

♻️ Avoid Single-use Plastic Carry a reusable water bottle, especially around ghats and heritage sites.
🛍️ Support Local Artisans Buy genuine Chikankari in Lucknow, Agra marble inlay, and Banarasi silk weavers.
🧹 Respect Sacred Waters Keep Ganga, Yamuna, and Sarayu ghats litter-free and honor local rituals.
🚶 Green Local Transit Prefer electric e-rickshaws, metro systems, and walking trails in old city cores.
