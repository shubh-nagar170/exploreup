Travel Responsibly

Sustainable Travel in UP

Help preserve Uttar Pradesh’s centuries-old monuments, sacred rivers, and natural habitats for future generations.

Avoid Single-use PlasticCarry a reusable water bottle, especially around ghats and heritage sites.
Support Local ArtisansBuy genuine Chikankari in Lucknow, Agra marble inlay, and Banarasi silk weavers.
Respect Sacred WatersKeep Ganga, Yamuna, and Sarayu ghats litter-free and honor local rituals.
Green Local TransitPrefer electric e-rickshaws, metro systems, and walking trails in old city cores.
